## A useful reference session

1. Pick a concrete question, such as whether the objective is readable on a phone.
2. Open the matching board, compare examples and play its clips.
3. Write what you observed separately from what you infer.
4. Make an original small prototype, then compare it on the target device.
5. Record your result and link the source board. Do not treat tutorial illustrations as evidence about a particular game's implementation.

## Add your own captures

Use [[Visual Reference Template]] for a screenshot or clip. Record creator, source, capture date, permission or license, what is directly visible, what remains uncertain and what you plan to test. Include only media you may redistribute; otherwise link to the original. For motion, include enough context to assess anticipation, action and recovery, rather than a single dramatic frame.
