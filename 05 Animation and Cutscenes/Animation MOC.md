# Animation MOC

Map of content for animation, camera and cutscene knowledge. Start with principles and tools, then pick the movement or combat note that matches the feature being built, and finish with the Luau implementation note. Animations are one of the cheapest ways to make a game feel polished, and top games such as [[The Strongest Battlegrounds]], [[Jujutsu Shenanigans]], [[Rivals]] and [[Fisch]] lean heavily on custom clips.
