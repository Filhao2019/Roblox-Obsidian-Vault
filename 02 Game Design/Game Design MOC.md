# Game Design MOC

Map of the `02 Game Design/` folder: what makes top Roblox games spread, loop, retain and earn, grounded in the 27 games of [[Top Games Chart 2026-11-02]] and Roblox's official creator docs. Read in the suggested order if you are designing a new game; jump to a single note if you are fixing one metric.

## Suggested reading order

1. [[What Makes a Roblox Game Go Viral]] — how games spread (short-form video, brainrot memes, stealing hooks, streamers, update hype) with case studies.
2. [[Core Gameplay Loops]] — minute/session/long-term loops and the loop of every genre in the chart, with diagrams.
3. [[Steal and Collect Tycoon Formula]] — the dominant 2025–26 genre: loop, base defense, offline income, rarity tables, why it spreads.
4. [[Onboarding and First Session]] — first 60 seconds, Roblox's onboarding techniques, FTUE metrics.
5. [[Retention and Progression Systems]] — D1/D7/D30, daily rewards, rebirth, passes, collections, events, offline earnings.
6. [[Monetization Patterns]] — passes, products, subscriptions, private servers, gacha policy, DevEx and ethics.
7. [[Social Features and Multiplayer Hooks]] — trading, friends, parties, social proof, server size, safety.
8. [[Thumbnails Icons and Titles]] — icon/thumbnail/title patterns from the chart and Roblox's personalization tools.
9. [[Live Ops and Update Cadence]] — weekly drops, admin-abuse events, countdowns, holiday events, calendar template.
10. [[Roblox Discovery Algorithm and Metrics]] — ranking signals, analytics, Creator Rewards, DevEx, Ads Manager.
11. [[What Every Roblox Game Needs]] — launch checklist: gameplay, UI, data saving, analytics, mobile, thumbnails, compliance.
12. [[Genre Playbooks]] — one short playbook per genre on the chart.
13. [[Game Design References]] — categorized link library of every source found.

## Evidence conventions

- Roblox docs and staff posts are authoritative for rules and numbers; press and trackers are used for case studies and are marked `(unverified)` when single-sourced.
- Design-target numbers (for example rarity tables or retention goals) are *planning ranges*, not measurements of any specific game.
- Chart numbers change hourly; the Q3 2026 platform figures come from press summaries because the investor deck could not be text-extracted.
