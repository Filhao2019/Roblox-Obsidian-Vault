# Reference Driven Roblox Workflow

Use this workflow when a feature needs a clear visual target before Roblox implementation. It connects the vault's research and media to a small, reviewable build brief. These steps are an original workflow inspired by the category-first organization of [[Roblox AI External Library]].

## 1. Define the decision

State one outcome: a readable shop, a convincing idle, a clear impact or a recognizable entrance. Record the target devices, input methods, rig, art direction and any measured budget. Leave unknown budgets explicitly undecided.

## 2. Select evidence

Fill [[Reference Selection Sheet]] with a small set of complementary examples: one for composition, one for interaction or motion, and one for materials or atmosphere. Prefer a few inspected examples over an entire unreviewed folder. Start at [[Visual References MOC]] for offline material or [[Roblox AI External Library]] for external discovery.

For each selection, write what is directly visible and the specific idea you want to adapt. Separate interpretation from observation. For motion or audio, record a clip range and what was actually played or heard. A still cannot prove timing, and an image cannot prove audio quality.

## 3. Write the contract

Choose a brief from [[Reference Workflows MOC]]. Specify the result, reference roles, original design choices, Explorer or asset structure, interaction states and evidence needed to accept the change. Match the project's real synchronization route and tools. If source files are available but live Studio access is not, label the deliverable accordingly.

## 4. Build one complete example

Implement a small path end to end, such as opening a shop and showing an unaffordable item state. Include cleanup and restoration behavior where cameras, sound, inputs or effects change. Expand only after the path works.

## 5. Compare and record

Compare screenshots at the intended sizes and motion at normal speed. Exercise keyboard, touch and controller paths that the project targets. Record what passed, what failed and what was not run. Preserve the exact source links and document why the result suits your own game.
