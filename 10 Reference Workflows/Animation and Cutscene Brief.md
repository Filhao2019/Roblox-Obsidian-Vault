# Animation and Cutscene Brief

A reusable brief for one short motion or camera sequence. This is an original planning tool; no runnable system or tested Studio implementation is supplied by this note.

## Prepare the references

Choose a pose or framing reference and a real motion clip when timing matters. Record the rig, body proportions, camera framing, action phases and exact clip ranges inspected. Keep your character, movement and scene composition original.

Example task: a receptionist greeting followed by a brief dialogue camera. Define when players retain movement and when they may skip or interrupt the sequence.

## Ask for a concrete deliverable

Key poses or a storyboard, timing notes, rig and animation ownership assumptions, implementation responsibilities and restoration behavior for camera, controls and UI. Include behavior for skip, death, respawn and scene interruption when those can occur.

## Acceptance review

Inspect from gameplay angles, check looping or transition boundaries, test interruptions and confirm the camera and controls restore. A storyboard review is not a played animation test. Mark uploaded animation IDs and Studio playback as unverified until checked.
