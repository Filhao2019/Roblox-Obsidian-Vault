An external discovery guide to izzyfrm's Roblox AI library. It complements this vault's attributed offline study boards with broader visual inspiration. The folders below are online references; their media is not bundled in this vault.

## What was verified

These counts measure files, not quality or unique artwork. No root license file, plugin manifest, skill file or executable package was found in that tree. ## How to combine it with this vault

Browse a category, open a few relevant files and record their exact links in [[Reference Selection Sheet]]. Attach the actual images when your AI tool supports them, or explicitly ask the agent to open and inspect the selected files. A folder URL alone does not mean an agent has seen the pictures.

Compare the selection with the relevant offline study board and use [[Reference Driven Roblox Workflow]] to create an original brief. Inspect individual images before describing their visual content: this guide verifies directory structure, not every picture. Static animation or effect sheets cannot establish timing, and SFX images cannot establish sound quality.
