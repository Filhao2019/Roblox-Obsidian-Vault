# Style and Environment Brief

A reusable brief for a cohesive room or small exterior. This is an original planning tool; no runnable system or tested Studio implementation is supplied by this note.

## Prepare the references

Choose separate references for architecture, material response and lighting. Record major shapes, repeated modules, palette and focal points. Describe what should be original: layout, props, signage and narrative details.

Example direction: a warm period-hotel reception with walnut, aged brass and cream walls. Treat this as one project example, not a default for other games. Establish a clear guest route and an unobstructed view to reception before adding decoration.

## Ask for a concrete deliverable

A small blockout; a material and lighting specification; asset names, dimensions, pivots and placement; collision decisions; source/export locations; and screenshots from player height. Identify which details belong in geometry versus textures.

## Acceptance review

Check door clearance and navigation with the intended avatar, silhouettes at gameplay distance, lighting readability and collision. Measure performance on the target device before claiming the scene fits its budget.
