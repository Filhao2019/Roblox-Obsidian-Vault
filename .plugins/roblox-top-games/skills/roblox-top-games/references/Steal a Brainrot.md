# Steal a Brainrot

Steal a Brainrot is the game that named the brainrot wave. Players buy Italian-brainrot meme units off a conveyor, place them in a base for passive `$/s`, and steal from other players' bases while theirs is unlocked. It was released on 16 May 2025 by Brazilian Spyder (SpyderSammy and do_small), published under Do Big Studios. Peak concurrency is reported around 25M (unverified). Its lesson is how far a tiny loop goes with a social risk and a timer on top. See [[Brainrot Games Roundup]] for its competitors.

## Core gameplay loop
- **Minute:** watch the conveyor, buy the best unit you can afford and walk it to a free slot. Then defend the base or run to an unlocked one.
- **Session:**
  - Your base locks for 30 s on join.
  - After that you can relock for 60 s, plus 10 s per rebirth.
  - A carried stolen unit slows the thief and alerts the owner. One bat hit returns it.
- **Long-term:** rebirth for a permanent multiplier and more slots or floors. Fill the Index for mutation-completion bonuses.

## Why it went viral
- Meme IP that kids already knew from short-video feeds.
- A PvP theft loop with a clear counter (lock timer + bat) that produces clips.
- The conveyor acts like a free gacha on every join, with pity spawns: a Legendary about every 5 min and a Mythic about every 15 min in public servers.
- Frequent admin-abuse events and limited modes (Tsunami Mode, Jan 2026) spike concurrency.

## Progression and retention
- Rarity ladder (one guide): Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Brainrot God, Secret, OG.
- Income per tier: Common about $1/s; Legendary about 200–1,800/s; Mythic about 1.4k–16k/s; God above 50k/s; Secret in the millions.
- About 14 mutations, including Gold, Diamond, Rainbow, Galaxy and Candy. Only one mutation per unit; traits stack on top.
- Fuse Machine, Craft Machine, Lucky Blocks and the Index (completion gives a base colour and a multiplier).

## Monetization
- Event-driven rare spawns, Lucky Blocks and fuse or craft systems. Exact gamepass and product list not found (unverified).

## Art direction and 3D assets
- Meme-character meshes on simple plots. The exact build method and polycount are not documented (unverified). Wikipedia describes the characters as voxel-style.
- See [[Voxel and Brainrot Model Style]] for how to make an original roster in this style.

## UI and GUI
- The HUD is centred on cash and `$/s`. Overhead tags above each unit show name, rarity, mutation and income. A countdown sits on the base lock.
- Fonts are disputed: a Fonts In Use entry credits Comic Sans, Source Sans and Montserrat, but a commenter there says the purchase font is Builder Sans.
- Full kit and Luau tag example: [[Brainrot and Simulator UI Patterns]].

## Lessons to steal
- Show the money rate everywhere.
- Give victims a counter-play and a timer.
- Use pity timers on random spawns.
- Ship mutations as materials, not new models.
- Invent your own characters.
