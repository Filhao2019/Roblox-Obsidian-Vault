# Roblox Top 50 UI Study Ranks 31-50 2026-11-19

Twenty more experiences from Roblox's Top Playing Now chart, continuing [[Roblox Top 30 UI Study 2026-11-19]] in the same per-screen format. Twelve entries have an inspected in-game UI capture; eight have only store art and are labelled so they are not used as HUD references. Use [[Roblox UI Patterns from the Top 30]] to pick references by player task, and [[Figma Workflow for Roblox UI]] when rebuilding a screen.

## Snapshot and evidence

- **Rank.** [Timestamped chart snapshot](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts), labelled November 19, 2026 at 20:19 UTC, mirroring [Roblox Top Playing Now](https://www.roblox.com/charts/top-playing-now) (worldwide, computer). Five titles in ranks 31–50 were already covered by the Top 30 study (The Strongest Battlegrounds, Evade, Violence District, King Legacy, Ant Evolution), so the next five uncovered titles were taken from Roblox's live Top Playing Now sort fetched later the same day. Those rows are tagged `live`; their player counts come from that fetch.
- **Heading numbers** run 31–50 in order. The table gives the actual chart rank. Rank 39 shows fewer players than rank 40 exactly as the snapshot publishes it.
- **Review.** Every listed capture was viewed in a browser, not downloaded. Captures come from guide articles and Roblox game-page media and may predate the chart date. Layout claims not seen in a capture are marked (unverified).
- **Videos.** Linked YouTube videos are search results or guide embeds and were not watched; each says so.
- **Measurements.** W and H are rough visual estimates as a percentage of the pictured frame. Colours are named by appearance. See [[Media Attribution and Reuse]] for vault conventions.

### 33. Illegal Soccer

**Screen.** In-match view from a tutorial frame (allthings.how guide) plus a bloxodes match overview and item shop. Top-left: Roblox menu pill (3 icons). Bottom-center: a dark checklist card ("Kick done / Slide into them to trip them") with a green play arrow. Right edge, stacked from the middle down: small action chips Kick, Pass, Tackle, Lob, Request, each with a key letter. A separate frame shows the shop: a centered wide white panel with tabs "Daily Shop / Your Items", a coin counter top-right of the panel, an item grid of 10 cards (2 rows of 5), a green Buy/roll button on the right, and a bottom bar with Shop, Inventory, Friends. A match frame shows a tiny scoreboard "0 : 0" top-center with a red and blue score chip.

**Size.** Right-edge action chip stack ≈9% of frame width and ≈20% of height. Shop panel ≈80% of frame width and ≈50% of height.

**Color.** Dark translucent card with white text and a mint-green accent; the active chip is white (inactive chips are greyed). Shop panel is warm off-white with colored item icons; scoreboard chip is red and blue on dark.

**Design takeaway.** Putting the key-labelled action chips on the right edge and lighting only the active one makes controls self-teaching without a separate help screen. Pitfall: the white shop panel is bright against a dark arena and the item text is tiny at this size.

## Cross-game patterns

- **Corner-anchored resources.** Boost rings, cash readouts and bars sit in corners (Turbo Soccer boost bottom-right, Net a Fish cash bottom-left, STAND OUT health top-left); the center stays clear for play.
- **Right-edge action stacks.** Illegal Soccer chips and STAND OUT skill list sit on the right edge, numbered or key-labelled, with the active one highlighted.
- **Top-center timers and scores.** Tower of Hell, 13 Days and Turbo Soccer each put one compact timer/score cluster at top-center (≈8-18% of frame width).
- **Centered modal panels for shop and rebirth.** Shops and menus are centered rectangles (≈35-80% of width) over a dimmed world: Illegal Soccer shop, Tower of Hell gears, Net a Fish Rebirth.
- **One accent color per screen.** Yellow save buttons, green cash chips, orange FORGE button; the rest is dark or neutral.
- **Tutorial text in-world, not in a menu.** Illegal Soccer uses an on-screen checklist card, STAND OUT a quest line under the health bar.
- **Pitfall shared by many.** Tiny text and tiny touch targets (Turbo Soccer settings, Net a Fish hotbar, 13 Days suspicion bar).
- **Tabbed, color-coded modals.** Huss Valley (SHOP/ABILITIES/ITEMS tabs, gold/green/blue cards) and Creatures of Sonaria (Event Missions tabs) use a tab row with a distinct color per tab or card and a red close X top-right.
- **Borrowed menus and phone launchers.** Unstable SMP copies the Minecraft main-menu button stack; LifeTogether RP puts its menu in an in-world phone with an Avatar Editor panel.
- **In-world feedback text.** Volley Bomb shows timing feedback ("TOO LOW.") as floating colored text instead of a HUD element.
- **Caveat.** 8 of the 20 entries still lack a real HUD capture, so these patterns come from the 12 games with one.
