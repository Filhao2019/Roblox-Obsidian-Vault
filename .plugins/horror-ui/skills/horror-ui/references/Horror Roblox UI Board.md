# Horror Roblox UI Board

209 in-game screenshots from 8 games, collected from each game's Fandom wiki. Low-contrast, diegetic and minimal HUDs: stamina and health bars, generator and objective prompts, lobbies, shops and death screens. Forsaken leads; Dead by Daylight is the asymmetric-horror reference Forsaken echoes. Wiki screenshots are fan captures and may show older versions; verify in-game before copying a detail.

Images are **hotlinked** from Fandom (static.wikia.nocookie.net); they are not stored or redistributed here, and you need an internet connection to see them. Sources: `_Data/reference-media-2026-11-20.json`. Copy patterns, never assets.
