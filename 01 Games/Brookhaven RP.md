# Brookhaven RP

Brookhaven RP is an open-ended town roleplay: no levels, quests or main currency, just houses, cars, jobs-as-costumes and emotes. Created by solo developer Wolfpaq in April 2020, it overtook Adopt Me in 2023 to become the most-visited Roblox experience and was acquired by Voldex on Feb 4, 2025 (per a tracker). With ~89 billion visits and a 30-player cap it shows how far a sandbox with zero goals can go. Learn from it: instant start, social stage-building and weekly content drops.

## Core gameplay loop
- Minute to minute: spawn in the city, pick a house or vehicle, roleplay with strangers or friends, change outfits, use emotes.
- Session loop: explore new locations, visit friends' houses, try new vehicles from a weekly update.
- Long loop: buy gamepasses for premium houses and vehicles; participate in seasonal events. A review notes the lack of structure is also a criticism (aimless once novelty fades).

## Why it went viral
- Zero onboarding: players start within about a minute (per a tracker article).
- It launched in April 2020 as lockdowns pushed kids online (tracker narrative, unverified).
- Open sandbox works as a stage for roleplay content on TikTok and YouTube, including viral "Roblox is deleting Brookhaven" rumors that the press debunked.
- Weekly updates, normally Fridays, keep returning players seeing something new.
- Primary audience reportedly ages 9-12.

## Progression and retention
- No XP or levels. Content-based progression: 115+ properties, 190+ vehicles, 80+ cosmetic job roles (reported counts), emotes.
- Seasonal events: Winter Olympics (Feb 2026), Cherry Blossom update (Mar 2026) with a free themed house and a premium estate, Trucks and Trailers (Mar 20, 2026), Easter event.
- Avatar editor update with makeup and undo (from the game description).

## Monetization
- Free to play; about 24 free vehicles; premium vehicles and properties via gamepasses roughly 275-799 Robux; estate-tier houses cost more. Combined passes about 5,700 Robux per a tracker.
- Private-server features.
- Estimated revenue ~$2.3M/month (low confidence).

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- Sell *places and vehicles*, not power; roleplay games thrive on property and cosmetic status.
- Never block the start with a tutorial.
- Weekly Friday-style drops train return habits.
- Allow players to be "roles" via costumes instead of building jobs systems.
- Support creator moments (house tours, drama).
