# Rivals

Rivals is a round-based first-person shooter launched by Nosniy Games in mid-2024. Duels run from 1v1 to 5v5, first to five rounds, with one life per round, a weapon-buying economy and an ELO ranked ladder, closer to CS2 or Valorant than to Roblox's older Arsenal-style FPS. It passed Arsenal as Roblox's most-visited FPS by May 2025, won an RIA25 Best Shooter award and now has about 19 billion visits and the platform's highest favorite count in this chart (96.5M). Learn from it: skill-based simplicity, a pad-based duel entry, and a cosmetic-only economy.

## Core gameplay loop
- Minute to minute: step on a duel pad to challenge someone or queue, play rounds with one life each, win five.
- Session loop: earn keys and contracts, open cases, equip skins, climb win streaks.
- Long loop: Season Pass, ranked ladder (Bronze up to Archnemesis, per trackers), new maps and weapons every update.

## Why it went viral
- Accessible on desktop, mobile, Xbox and PS5; PS5 availability credited with recent growth by a tracker.
- Simple pad duels in a shared lobby create spectators and challenge culture.
- Fast update cadence: 19 major updates in 21 months (tracker), plus Experimental Maps that rotate for feedback.
- Strong anti-cheat messaging in the description.
- Peak concurrents reported around 964-967k.

## Progression and retention
- 30 maps, around 49-52 weapons across primary, secondary, melee and utility, 21 modes (tracker).
- Skin Cases (249 Robux) and keys; contracts for free cosmetics; season pass with free and premium tracks.
- Ranked requires a high level (Level 50, later 100), identity verification, hidden MMR with a visible rank rating, map bans.
- Season 2 "Polar Season" Dec 2025, Season 3 "The Fame Season" launched Apr 24, 2026 with Grapple and Spear weapons and maps Westown, Studio and Museum.
- Update 24 is a Halloween update ("Rivalsween").

## Monetization
- Cosmetics only: skins, wraps, charms, finishers, emotes, bundles (for example a 824 Robux Prop Bundle, 599 and 999 Robux pass bundles). Trackers say no purchase gives a competitive edge.
- Estimated revenue ~$1.7M/month (tracker, moderate confidence).

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- Few rules, high skill ceiling; no abilities makes balance easier.
- A physical "step on pad to duel" entry turns the lobby into a spectacle.
- Gate ranked behind level and verification to protect ladder quality.
- Rotating Experimental Maps get free playtesting.
- Cosmetic cases plus contracts mix paid and free.
