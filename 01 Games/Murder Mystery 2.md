# Murder Mystery 2

Murder Mystery 2 (MM2) is a 2014 social-deduction round game: one player is secretly the Murderer, one is the Sheriff with a gun, everyone else is an Innocent. The Murderer kills with a knife; the Sheriff must identify and shoot them. It has been a top game for over a decade, with 32 billion visits, because rounds are short and the cosmetic knife/gun economy, with boxes, codes and trading, gives players a collection to chase. Learn from it: tiny rule set, cosmetics-only monetization and a trade economy.

## Core gameplay loop
- Minute to minute: lobby, map vote or random map, role assignment shown on screen, then hide, run, throw or shoot until the Murderer wins or dies.
- Session loop: earn coins during rounds, open knife or gun boxes, equip cosmetics, trade with others.
- Long loop: collect rare "Godly" and "Ancient" weapons and keep them as status symbols or trade stock.

## Why it went viral
- Easy to explain: hide, find the killer, shoot. Works for kids and for streamers.
- Reported history: the original Murder Mystery was renamed and replaced by MM2 in Dec 2014 (search result summary).
- Codes shared by the creator ("free knife codes" in the page description) give a reason to follow and subscribe.
- Rare weapon lore (for example Nik's Scythe, reportedly given to bug reporters and friends, only a handful of owners) fuels YouTube and trading content.
- Duplication glitch history (a 2018 duping glitch is mentioned by a fan source) shows how the economy drives interest.

## Progression and retention
- Rarity ladder (fan source): Common grey, then Uncommon, Rare, Legendary (orange per one list), Godly, Ancient and others; sources disagree on colour mapping, so verify in-game.
- A fan page says there are 86 Godlies (61 knives, 25 guns) and box Godlies drop around 1 in 500. Older limited items (Icewing, Ghostly, Ginger) become trade-only.
- Seasonal events (Halloween, Christmas) rotate limited weapons.
- Daily and event rewards, codes.

## Monetization
- Boxes and Robux-sold weapons, passes, and limited seasonal items; weapons have no gameplay advantage (damage is the same), so it is cosmetic.
- Older gamepass items become scarce, so purchase timing gets a collectible value.

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- A secret role gives huge replay value with very little content.
- Make cosmetics the monetization so the game stays fair.
- Let items get retired; scarcity feeds a trade economy.
- Reward the community with codes tied to creator channels.
- Keep a dropped-weapon mechanic so the round can flip.
