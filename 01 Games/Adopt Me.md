# Adopt Me

Adopt Me! began in 2017 as a family roleplay about adopting children and became a pet-raising, home-decorating and trading game after adoptable pets launched in summer 2019. Run by Uplift Games (Bethink as creative director, NewFissy as tech director), it passed 3 billion plays by late 2019, 10 billion in July 2020 and now sits above 45 billion visits. Learn from it: needs-based pet care as a gentle loop, a four-to-one merge mechanic (Neon, Mega Neon), and brand-safe trade systems.

## Core gameplay loop
- Minute to minute: hatch an egg, take care of a pet's needs (hunger, sleep, play, etc.), complete tasks to earn Bucks and pet growth.
- Session loop: age a pet to full grown, collect four same pets, fuse into Neon, use Bucks for furniture and decor.
- Long loop: Mega Neon pets, trade for legendary pets, seasonal events and building a showcase home.

## Why it went viral
- Pets arrived in summer 2019 and drove rapid growth (Wikipedia).
- April Fools event: a Pet Rock reportedly drew about 680k active players.
- Licensed tie-ins (Scoob!, Sing 2, Minions) in 2020-2022.
- Player counts reportedly roughly triple during major updates; peak around 1.9M (tracker).
- Trade culture and fake-value drama create endless YouTube content.

## Progression and retention
- Five rarities (common to legendary, per Dexerto), with colour or label cues (exact colours unverified).
- Pet life stages from newborn to full grown; Neon = 4 full-grown same pets; Mega Neon = 4 Neons.
- Egg types (Farm, Fossil, Christmas, Jungle, Cracked) and event-limited pets.
- Seasonal events with candy or gingerbread currencies; the Halloween event is live with a final 1.2x weekend and an event finale during "Admin Abuse".
- Anti-scam features introduced in Nov 2020: trade licenses for rare pets, viewable history, unfair-trade alerts.

## Monetization
- Robux-priced pets and eggs, gamepasses, bundles; Bucks earned in-game.
- Reported earnings about US$60 million a year at a 2021-era figure (Wikipedia), ~40 staff.
- Trade value of pets feeds off-platform black markets, which Uplift fights with licenses.

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- A 4-of-a-kind merge turns duplicates into progress.
- Care-taking tasks make a low-stress loop for young players.
- Time-limited events with a currency keep the game fresh without rebuilding systems.
- Build trade safety from the start (history, alerts, locks).
- Tie events to brands only when it adds a simple limited pet.
