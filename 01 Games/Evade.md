# Evade

A movement-based chase-survival game adapted from Garry's Mod "Nextbot Chase": meme-faced AI hunters (nextbots) roam large maps and down anything they touch, while players run, slide, bhop and revive each other until the round timer ends. It has stayed near the top for four years on a steady event/update cadence and a skill ceiling built entirely on movement tech. Learn from it: a cheap-to-author AI threat (a flat PNG that gets louder as it approaches), movement as the "weapon", and community-meme IP as a free marketing channel.

## Core gameplay loop
- **Minute-to-minute:** spawn in a map, sprint/slide/jump around geometry to break line of sight, avoid nextbots that chase by pathfinding, revive downed teammates (carry/revive mechanic), survive until the timer ends; last team/player standing wins.
- **Session loop:** choose a mode/map vote, play 5-10 minute rounds (unverified length), earn cash/XP, spend on perks, usables (deployables), cosmetics and emotes.
- **Long-term loop:** weekly missions, battle pass, collectables unlocking lore, new "Voucher" currency for quick revives/respawns (per the current game description), and seasonal events (Halloween "The Hollow" with 15 tickets across 7 special round collabs).

## Why it went viral
- Nextbot meme format already viral on TikTok/YouTube; a Know Your Meme entry traces the August 2022 surge to a YouTuber video and a TikTok trend of revive clips set to a sped-up song (115M visits by 2022-08-30).
- Pure movement skill = highlight-reel clips (bhop, emote-dash, slide-jumps).
- Collaborations: the 2026 Halloween event crosses over with other top games (DOORS is one of the special round collabs).
- Fully free; cheating draws unappealable bans, keeping the competitive scene healthy.

## Progression and retention
- Perks and usables you buy and upgrade (the current update added deployable and utility upgrades enhanced with tokens and XP).
- Weekly missions give "exotic" items and currencies; battle pass; tickets and collectables for events; shop, battlepass, settings and community UI were revamped and the codebase rewritten (per game description).
- Event calendar irregular but frequent; no fixed schedule published by trackers.

## Monetization
- Battle pass, premium cosmetics, VIP-server menu (owner-controlled), currency packs and voucher/revive products (patterns only).
- Revive tokens monetise the moment of failure without blocking free play.

## Art direction and 3D assets
- Mixed palette of environments: a city-inspired map, the Backrooms (yellow walls, pale fluorescent light), Quake-style blockout maps and Halloween event maps. ## Systems and code worth studying
- Pathfinding AI that hunts nearest player with speed ramps, round/map vote state machine, revive/carry network sync, movement tech (momentum-preserving slide/bhop with custom controller). → [[Round-Based and Horror Systems]] · [[Coding MOC]]

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- Make the threat audio-first: volume = distance, so players learn by ear.
- Let movement tech be the progression of skill, not only gear.
- Cheap AI threats + rotating "special rounds" produce endless content.
- Collaborate with neighbouring top games for cross-promotion.
- Sell revives/respawn vouchers: failure monetisation that is optional.
- Use event tags in the game title and a ticket-based event meta-loop.
