# Pressure

A supplementary horror reference selected for comparison with [[Doors]]. The official page provides strong tone and audio-oriented positioning, but limited evidence of individual gameplay systems. This is a source-scoped research profile with an original study plan; no playthrough or source inspection was performed.

## Confirmed from the official page

The [official listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/12411473842/Pressure) names Urbanshade: Hadal Division as creator, frames the player as expendable, recommends headphones/high graphics, describes the game as alpha, and advertises a group discount in the Dead-Drop Shop. These statements were available on 2026-11-02. They establish advertised positioning, not a measured player experience.

No current player counts, visit figures or server sizes were collected. Pressure is a supplementary reference, not a new entry in the dated Top Playing Now snapshot. A no-running-experiences message in a minimal web response is not sufficient evidence of an outage.

## Original comparative study plan

| Question | What to capture in a future session | Why it helps an original project |
|---|---|---|
| How does the opening establish the player's role? | First instructions, first controllable moment and first objective | Translate tone into a clear task |
| How are hazards taught? | Warning, response, failure and retry sequence | Evaluate whether failure creates useful knowledge |
| How does audio carry information? | Paired audio/visual signals with timestamps | Design accessible warnings and readable alternatives |
| What happens between threats? | A calm segment and the next escalation | Plan pacing with room to recover |
| What is the shop's role? | Offers, prices, purchase outcome and failure states | Distinguish convenience from necessary progression |

These are research questions, not confirmed descriptions of encounters. Exact room generation, creature behavior, UI layout, progression, animation, VFX, purchase conditions and retention metrics remain unverified.

## Build exercise

Write an original one-room encounter brief with one clear objective, two independent warning cues, a response action and a recoverable failure state. Define who owns state and how two simultaneous interactions resolve. Use [[Feature Build Contract]], [[Networking and Remotes]] and [[Security and Anti-Exploit]]. Compare the completed prototype with observations recorded later; do not copy game characters, sounds, names or assets.
