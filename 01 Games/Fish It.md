# Fish It

Fish It is an incremental fishing simulator by Fish Atelier (founder credited as Wildes): click to charge a cast, click fast to reel, sell the catch, buy better rods, unlock islands, and chase extremely rare fish expressed as "1 in X". The Roblox description says it was inspired by Fisch and Fishing Simulator. It is a good study of how a simplified, AFK-friendly fishing loop can outgrow its inspiration; its auto-fishing drives very long sessions. Compare [[Fisch]] and see [[Fishing Systems]].

## Core gameplay loop
- Minute to minute: click to charge, click quickly to reel (Roblox description); an auto-fish mode keeps casting while you are away.
- Session: sell fish for coins, upgrade rods, buy bait and boats, move to new islands (eight islands listed), dive for underwater secrets, enchant rods with stones, craft charms (Lava Basin expansion).
- Long term: hunt Secret and Forgotten fish, fill an aquarium (customizable in the latest update), join fishing crews and group events.

## Why it went viral
- Easy entry and passive progression: averages cited at 77 to 92 minutes per session (RoWatcher), which Roblox's recommendation system rewards.
- Rarity as bragging: Secret tier about 1 in 4 million, Forgotten tier about 1 in 25 million (Retro Island update) makes "I caught a Forgotten" the clip.
- Peak around 2.7 million concurrent players per most tracker sources (one RoWatcher sidebar says 1.4M on 2025-11-24; unresolved).
- Admin events (e.g. the Talon event: a roughly 10-minute window for a Robux-themed Secret) create stream-sniping moments.
- Weekly Sunday updates, battle passes and holiday events (Halloween: Forgotten fish, Halloween pets, aquarium customization; fishing crews).

## Progression and retention
- Six rod tiers from Common to Mythic; luck from rods, bait, enchants, charms and gamepass multipliers.
- 1,000,000+ fish variations from species x mutation x modifier (Roblox page).
- Event passes with free and paid tiers, limited stock skins, and limited battle pass items.
- New islands and tier additions each update (Classic Event: 42 Classic fish and 2 Secrets; Lava Basin Feb 2026: 15 species; Pirate Event: 35+ fish; Retro Island: 18+ species with a Forgotten and a Secret; Talon update: aquarium themes).

## Monetization
- Gamepasses: Advanced Luck reported at 545 Robux for 150% luck; VIP, Diving Kit, boats, double XP. Mystery crates with rod skins. Classic Event Pass with a paid tier around 3,876 Robux to skip ahead (RoWatcher). Estimated monthly revenue about 3 million USD (tracker estimate; low confidence).
- Many players buy third-party items for real money (a risk the developer cannot control).

## Art direction and 3D assets
- Bright cartoon island scenes with stylised low-poly fish; fish models show mutation recolours and glows. Each island has a distinct palette (retro island, lava basin, pirate cove, ghost-themed Halloween area). Guide sites identify fishing spots such as Lost Isle with a Sisyphus statue for Secrets. ## Systems and code worth studying
- Charge-and-click minigame (a spam counter against a progress bar), auto-fish with throttle, weighted drop tables with luck, enchant stone and charm crafting, aquarium persistence, crews/group event syncing. See [[Fishing Systems]] and [[Data Saving and Player Profiles]].

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- Show odds as "1 in X": it turns every catch into a number players can compare.
- Provide an AFK loop to lengthen sessions, but keep active play meaningfully better.
- Use time-limited admin events with short windows for social hype.
- Reveal new tiers above the old top (Secret then Forgotten) to extend the chase without invalidating older goals.
- Weekly fixed-day updates build habit.
- Watch the like ratio (82.8%): aggressive monetization and bots/exploits can drag it.
