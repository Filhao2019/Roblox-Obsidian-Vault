## Confirmed positioning and player-facing hooks

The [official game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/6516141723/DOORS?placeId=6516141723&universeId=2440500124) describes a horror experience built around doors, recommends learning through deaths, and recommends headphones. It warns about loud sounds/flashing lights. It also advertises a group-related revive and UGC-related Knobs/revive incentives. These are advertised offers at review time, not a complete economy audit.

## Core-loop study hypothesis

Original interpretation to investigate: uncertainty → cue → response → consequence → better knowledge on the next attempt. The official learn-from-death framing supports studying this pattern; exact encounter timing and failure rules still need direct observation.

For your own game, make one hazard understandable before adding more variants. Ask whether a failed player can describe what happened and a different action to try. Test this with a new player rather than assuming confusion creates replay value.

## Art, UI, animation, audio and VFX study plan

| Area | Capture during a future playthrough | Reusable decision to test |
|---|---|---|
| Environment | Player-height approach to an exit | Separate route landmarks from decoration |
| UI | Before danger, during failure and after returning | Keep recovery choices understandable |
| Animation/camera | Start, interruption and end of an encounter | Preserve control except when framing needs it |
| Audio | Warning onset versus the player response window | Pair an audible warning with another readable cue |
| VFX | Full effect versus low graphics view | Preserve the information when decoration is reduced |
