# Dress To Impress

A themed fashion-competition game: each round gives a theme, players have minutes in a dressing room, then walk a runway while everyone else rates them with 1-5 stars. It took dress-up, the most under-served big audience on Roblox (older girls/teens), and turned it into a game with a clear loop, social judging, TikTok-able outfits and pop-culture collabs. Learn from it: the "UGC-as-gameplay" loop where the player's own creation is the content.

## Core gameplay loop
- **Minute-to-minute:** vote/receive a theme, wardrobe browsing and layering (clothes, hair, makeup, patterns, colors, skin, nails) in a fixed time (about five to six minutes per sources), runway walk with poses, rate others 1-5 stars, podium for the top three.
- **Session loop:** repeat rounds with changing themes (the community wiki lists ~784 themes); optional modes: Style Showdown (10-player elimination, added March 2025), Duos, Freeplay (no timers).
- **Long-term loop:** career star ranking (14-tier rank ladder toward 150K+ stars), Cash for items, codes, VIP, seasonal items and collabs.

## Why it went viral
- TikTok/Twitch-native: "outfit of the day" parodies, theme-challenge clips, celebrity-look recreations (Met Gala, Grammys).
- Pop-culture collabs: Charli XCX "Brat" (Aug 2024; 651K concurrent in one session), Lady Gaga x Mayhem (Aug 2025), Wicked: For Good (Nov 2025), Flee the Facility crossover (Jan 2026), plus a physical toy line.
- Creator story: a teen developer (Gigi) began at 14; the mystery of the staff and a lore storyline (Lana, missing people, hidden clues) fuel Reddit theories.
- Nostalgia from Fashion Famous-style games for the same audience.
- Peak around 1.5M concurrent and three 2024 Roblox Innovation Awards per rowatcher; it is credited with doubling Roblox streaming viewership.

## Progression and retention
- 14-tier rank ladder and star stats; hundreds of themes and 1,140 catalogued clothing items, 257 of them VIP-gated; codes (26 active at time of crawl) unlock items rather than Cash.
- Seasonal "updates" (19 listed seasons), Halloween 2026 started Oct 17 adding at least 12 clothing items.
- Lore/ARG layer keeps lapsed players checking new updates.

## Monetization
- VIP (reported 299 Robux monthly or 799 permanent): extra closet items, exclusive hairs/outfits; a community wiki says VIP carries no hard sales pressure.
- Robux-priced items and Cash-priced items; gifting VIP; collab-themed lobbies and items.
- Free access keeps the player base and spectator content huge.

## Art direction and 3D assets
- Bright, clean boutique/runway: pastel pink and neon accents, glossy backstage, a small stage map with modest music. More detailed character animation than most Roblox games, giving a "lifelike" feel. Clothing is layered 3D accessories plus 2D classic textures; the community treats clipping as a creative tool. → [[Art Styles of Top Games]]

## UI and GUI
- Dressing-room screen with category tabs (tops, bottoms, shoes, hair, makeup etc.) and color/pattern pickers, bold sans type, rounded pill buttons in pink/white (unverified exact colors).
- Rating bar of 1-5 stars at the runway; countdown timer; podium result screen. A commentator noted that the UI is simple enough that new players grasp the loop quickly. → [[UI Breakdown of Top Games]]

## Animation
- Runway walk and pose animations, fashion-show pose picker, celebratory podium animations, emote-based dances; Lady Gaga collab added themed avatar cutscenes. → [[Animation MOC]]

## SFX and music
- Light pop/electronic runway tracks, rating chimes, crowd-like cheers (unverified); collab seasons swap in themed music. → [[SFX of Top Games]]

## Systems and code worth studying
- Round timer with theme broadcast, avatar-appearance application of layered accessories (HumanoidDescription), per-viewer vote aggregation, rank/star persistence. → [[Coding MOC]] · [[Social Features and Multiplayer Hooks]]

## Lessons to steal (patterns, not content)
- Make the player's creation the content; the judges are other players.
- Pick a trend-driven prompt (theme) each round so every round is a new creative problem.
- Plan collabs that give the game new lobby/stage skins, not just items.
- Rank ladders attached to a neutral stat (stars) give visible prestige without pay-to-win.
- Sell optional breadth (VIP wardrobe) not victory.
- A lore side-layer (hidden clues) gives passive community engagement.
