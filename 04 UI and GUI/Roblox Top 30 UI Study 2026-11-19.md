## Per-game references

### 1. Steal An Egg

Related profile: [[Steal An Egg]].

**Screen.** Base and collection HUD. Outlined, chunky icon-and-label buttons at both sides. Two resource totals sit low left, with the hotbar and event clock along the bottom.

**Size.** Shop button ≈9% W × 7% H. Hotbar ≈30% W.

**Color.** Lime Shop, cyan Index, orange paw, white labels with dark strokes.

**Design takeaway.** Pair resource glyphs with stable positions. Watch for unrelated saturated buttons competing with the main action.

[Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/107778070777162) · [Inspected capture](https://files.bo3.gg/uploads/image/129278/image/webp-d456cb4a1beeb999c5591b272c0282da.webp) · [Capture source](https://bo3.gg/games/articles/steal-an-egg-pets-guide)

### 2. Adopt Me!

Related profile: [[Adopt Me]].

**Screen.** Touch backpack open. Low-center backpack with a category grid, search, a scrollable item grid and selected-category fill. Touch controls remain in the corners.

**Size.** Backpack ≈42% W × 38% H. Bottom item circles ≈8% W across.

**Color.** Pink frame and header, white content, green category tiles, orange selected tab, red close.

**Design takeaway.** Keep filter categories and items visibly separate. Give selection a second cue such as a label or border.

[Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/920587237) · [Inspected capture](https://media.karousell.com/media/photos/products/2025/12/22/adopt_me_pets_pet_wears_ride_p_1766407664_ea53a8a5.jpg) · [Capture source](https://www.carousell.sg/p/adopt-me-pets-pet-wears-ride-potions-1411206184/)

### 3. Blox Fruits

Related profile: [[Blox Fruits]].

**Screen.** Early quest and mastery. Quest and resource stack on the left, mastery and skill requirements on the right. The character and route stay visible in the center.

**Size.** Each yellow action ≈9% W × 5% H. Health bar ≈19% W.

**Color.** Gold menu, violet XP, green health and cyan energy over dark neutral panels.

**Design takeaway.** Assign each persistent meter a stable label and position. Put unavailable skills and their unlock requirements in the same list.

[Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/2753915549) · [Inspected capture](https://cms-cdn.now.gg/cms-media/2024/06/BloxFruits_Maps_EN_3-1024x576.jpg) · [Capture source](https://now.gg/blog/game-guides/blox-fruits-maps-guide-en.html)

### 4. Throw Food For Animals

Related profile: [[Throw Food For Animals]].

**Screen.** New-animal reveal around 0:02. Stacked reveal puts rarity and odds above the animal, with variant, size and income below. A sticker marks the new collection entry.

**Size.** Central rarity headline ≈25% W. Odds line ≈21% W.

**Color.** Red-magenta rarity heading, white odds, yellow gold label, orange new sticker, dark outlines.

**Design takeaway.** In a reward reveal, separate the result, what made it rare, and what it changes. Give the player a moment to read the outcome.

[Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/120219252362013) · [BasicBlox gameplay, 0:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dq4m5qt1sEw&t=2s)

### 5. Brookhaven RP

Related profile: [[Brookhaven RP]].

**Screen.** Driving with props open. Large pictogram dock on the right with an adjacent prop grid. Vehicle controls form a separate top strip.

**Size.** Right-side dock ≈8% W. Open prop grid ≈21% W × 50% H.

**Color.** Translucent gray tiles, predominantly white or gray icons, cyan vehicle controls, red close.

**Design takeaway.** Keep frequent tools at the edge and expand the selected category inward. Test icon comprehension without prior game knowledge.

### 6. 99 Nights in the Forest

Related profile: [[99 Nights in the Forest]].

**Screen.** Forest gathering. Sparse survival HUD with day above, hunger at left, contextual sack count at the character and an Unstore prompt near the bottom-right.

**Color.** Charcoal tracks, orange hunger fill, white labels and selected-slot outline.

**Design takeaway.** Use a small persistent survival HUD. Show action-specific counts and inputs only when the action is relevant.
